title: "Prey: Michael Crichton"

Movie aliens often look like humans, but *slightly* different. Special ears, green skin, etc. In cinema, this makes perfect sense! Casting a human-like alien and applying makeup to said human is far, far easier than training a tuna or salamander and convincing it to act out a scene.

Yet books have no such constraints! And for over two billion years, all of Earth's organisms were eukaryotic. By some unknown mechanism, these organisms began to cooperate and specialize. One hypothesis is that larger cells might have forgotten to digest the smaller cells they consumed which led to the former offering protection and the latter offering services like photosynthesis!

Eventually, these organisms came together and specialized further. Entire groups of cells would perform a specific function. In humans, we have neurons, muscle cells, etc. In the novel *Prey*, Michael Crichton explores what might happen if such an organism was entirely man made.
